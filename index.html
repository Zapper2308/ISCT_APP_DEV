ABOUT ISCT GROUP

Established in 1996, ISCT Group connects technical education with practical industry requirements. We support students, professionals, colleges, universities and industry through specialised training, institutional partnerships and campus-based programmes.

With training centres in Dharwad, Hubballi and Bengaluru, and institutional services extending across India, we help build relevant technical skills, strengthen academic programmes and create industry-ready learning environments.

Our group includes:

Global Infotech — Technology and software solutions
Global Designs — Product design and engineering services
Global BIM Academy — BIM, digital construction and sustainable building skills
Global Edutech — Technical education, training and institutional development
Global Futuretec — Emerging technologies, AI/ML, Data and future-focused skills

Together, these verticals support students, professionals, colleges, universities and industry through practical training, technology services and academic–industry collaboration.

Why ISCT Group


Three Decades of Technical Education and Industry Engagement
Since 1996, ISCT Group has been helping students, professionals and institutions build relevant technical capabilities through practical training, expert guidance and industry-oriented initiatives.

Industry-Relevant Learning
 Our programmes are aligned with current technologies, professional practices and evolving industry requirements.

Practical and Application-Oriented
 We focus on hands-on learning, demonstrations, projects and real-world applications—not only theoretical instruction.

Academic and Industry Connect
 Our experience with colleges, universities and industry enables us to bridge the gap between academic learning and workplace expectations.

Experienced Technical Team
 Our trainers and domain experts bring strong experience across programming, IT, AI/ML, CAD/CAM/CAE, product design, BIM and emerging technologies.

Solutions for Institutions
 We support institutions through MoUs, Centres of Excellence, BoS activities, FDPs, workshops, skill development programmes and industry–academia initiatives.

Long-Term Institutional Support
 We aim to build continuing relationships with institutions through curriculum support, faculty development, student training, competitions, internships and technology initiatives.

With ISCT Group, institutions and learners gain more than training—they gain a long-term technical partner.
