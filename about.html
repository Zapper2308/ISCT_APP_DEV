Where Education Meets Industry

Empowering Engineering Education Since 1996

Established in 1996, ISCT Group has been working at the intersection of engineering education, industry technologies and professional skill development.

We support engineering students, faculty members, institutions and industry professionals through practical training, curriculum support, technology integration and industry-oriented learning initiatives.

With a strong training center presence in Dharwad, Hubballi and Bengaluru, and institutional services extending across India, ISCT Group helps bridge the gap between academic learning and the skills expected by modern industries.

Scope of Services

What We Do

Our work is built around four key functional areas supporting students, academia and industry.

Engineering Education & Training

Industry-relevant training in programming, design, engineering software and emerging technologies. Designed for college students and working professionals aiming for immediate career enhancement.

Institutional Development

Comprehensive support for academic MoUs, Centres of Excellence (COEs), Boards of Studies (BoS), specialized curriculum development and sustainable academic-industry initiatives.

Faculty & Professional Development

Faculty Development Programs (FDPs), industry upskilling workshops, skill-development programmes and practical learning experiences to keep faculty abreast of cutting-edge software.

Technology & Innovation

Specialized technology support in BIM, CAD/CAM/CAE, product design, digital construction, AI/ML, data analytics, full-stack software development and digital transformation.
