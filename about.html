About ISCT Group

Empowering Engineering Education Since 1996

Established in 1996, ISCT Group has been working at the intersection of engineering education, industry technologies and professional skill development.

We support engineering students, faculty members, institutions and industry professionals through practical training, curriculum support, technology integration and industry-oriented learning initiatives.

With a strong presence in Dharwad, Hubballi and Bengaluru, and institutional engagements across India, ISCT Group helps bridge the gap between academic learning and the skills expected by modern industries.

What We Do
Our work is built around four key areas:

Engineering Education and Training Industry-relevant training in programming, design, engineering software and emerging technologies.

Institutional Development Support for MoUs, Centres of Excellence, Boards of Studies, curriculum development and academic-industry initiatives.

Faculty and Professional Development FDPs, workshops, skill-development programmes and practical learning experiences for faculty members and professionals.

Technology and Innovation Support in BIM, CAD/CAM/CAE, product design, digital construction, AI/ML, data analytics, software development and other emerging technologies.

Our Group
ISCT Group brings together specialised divisions serving different areas of engineering education and technology:

Global Infotech – Programming, software development and engineering technologies
Global Designs – Design, engineering and technical services
Global BIM Academy – BIM, digital construction and infrastructure technologies
Global Edutech -IT, programming and digital skill development
Global Futuretec – Future-focused technology and engineering initiatives

Our Approach
We believe that effective engineering education must go beyond theoretical knowledge. Our programmes focus on:

Practical and project-based learning
Industry-relevant tools and workflows
Faculty confidence and capability building
Academic-industry collaboration
Continuous institutional support
Skills aligned with current and future career opportunities

Our Institutional Connect
Over the years, ISCT Group has worked with engineering institutions, universities and technical organisations includes

IIT Bombay, IIT Madras, IISc Bengaluru, IIT Dharwad
VTU, Anna University, JNTU
RVCE, BMSCE, DSU
Alliance University, REVA University, JSS STU, NIE
and 300+ institutions across India”.

Our institutional engagements include training programs, academic collaborations, curriculum initiatives, Centres of Excellence, FDPs, workshops and technology-based skill development.

ISCT Group continues to empower students, educators and institutions with relevant technologies, practical skills and meaningful industry connections - helping engineering education move from knowledge to capability.
